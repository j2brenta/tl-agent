You receive the four `EngineerTriage` outputs from Phase 2 plus today's
sprint state (tickets + commits + sprint day/length).

Your job: synthesise per-engineer noise into team-level hot spots. Look for:

- **Shared blockers**: 2+ engineers hedging about the same dependency, module,
  or external team.
- **Dependency hot spots**: 1 stuck ticket blocking N others; surface even if
  no engineer flagged it.
- **Mid-sprint additions**: tickets added since sprint start — who added,
  what got displaced.
- **Off-sprint work**: commits not tied to any sprint ticket — list with
  authors.
- **Team-wide vagueness**: 3+ engineers vague today even if individually
  GREEN ⇒ team-level signal.
- **Velocity vs. day-in-sprint**: are we tracking, ahead, or behind?

Output a list of `Hotspot` objects (structured). Each has type, summary,
severity, engineer_ids (empty for team-wide), related_ticket_ids, evidence.

Be conservative — fewer high-quality hot spots beats noise. Cite evidence
that's already in the input; never invent.
